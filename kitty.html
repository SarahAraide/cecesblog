02 Ladybug facts

  1. They're not bugs. Ladybugs are beetles, from a family called Coccinellidae.
  2. There are thousands of kinds. Around 6,000 species live all over the world.
  3. Some are pink. Ladybugs can be red, orange, yellow, black or pink, with spots, stripes or no spots at all.
  4. The colours are a warning. Bright red and black tells birds: "I taste terrible, don't even try."
  5. They have stinky knees. When scared, they ooze a smelly yellow liquid from their leg joints.
  6. Spots don't tell their age. The number of spots depends on the species, not on how old they are.
  7. Gardeners love them. One ladybug can eat dozens of aphids (tiny plant-eating insects) a day.
  8. They hibernate in piles. In winter, many huddle together in big groups to sleep it off.
