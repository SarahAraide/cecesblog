Cece Once Said is a debut collection of 83 poems, written from the other side of the darkest years of a young life. Moving through seven sections, from roots and inheritance, through confinement, love and ruin, descent, grief and loss, to grace and return, Cece writes about depression, self-harm, family, first love and grief with unflinching honesty and a stubborn search for light.

Written between childhood notebooks and engineering lectures, these poems hold softness and fire side by side: the tenderness of someone who sees good in people, and the ache of someone who has carried more than she should. It is a book for anyone fighting an invisible battle, and for anyone who loves someone who is.

Dedication To those with an invisible battle, you are very much seen.

03 Poems

Poems in the spirit of Emily Dickinson (opens in a new tab) and Charles Bukowski (opens in a new tab).

Content note This collection deals with suicidal thoughts, self-harm, abuse and grief. Please read gently. If you are struggling, you are not alone: ERAN emotional first aid is available 24/7 at 1201.

Soil

We rose from the same soil, you and I. I despise all that you are. Vicariously, through me, you climbed closer to the sun. You watched while I reached — thinner and thinner — burned at the edges as I leaned toward light. You became an observer; I became ash. We tried to nurture the same ground and failed. Maybe it’s impossible to grow away from what you taught me, while they feed me with the very soil you once tried to bury me under.

from Cece Once Said

04 About Cece

Cece is the pen name of Sarah Araide, a poet and mechanical engineering student at the Technion. She was born in Maghar and discovered writing at the age of seven, turning it into a way of understanding herself and the world around her.

Her poetry carries both softness and fire. She writes about pain, love, grief, identity and survival, and about the complicated act of returning to oneself. Poetry is where she gives shape to what cannot be solved by logic alone. Cece Once Said is her first collection.
