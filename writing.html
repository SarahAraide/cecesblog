02 Short stories to read

A reading list of short stories, all free to read online.

The Yellow Wallpaper

Charlotte Perkins Gilman · 1892

Told through a woman's secret journal during a "rest cure", as her mind slowly unravels. A classic about mental health, and about not being listened to.

Read it on Project Gutenberg (opens in a new tab)

The Story of an Hour

Kate Chopin · 1894

Two pages, one hour, a whole life turned upside down. One of the most famous short stories ever written.

Read it on the Kate Chopin International Society site (opens in a new tab)

Girl

Jamaica Kincaid · 1978

A mother's advice to her daughter, poured out in a single breathless sentence. It reads like a poem.

Read it in The New Yorker (opens in a new tab)

The Lottery

Shirley Jackson · 1948

A sunny village gathers for its yearly tradition. Famous for its ending, so don't look it up first.

Read it in The New Yorker (opens in a new tab)
