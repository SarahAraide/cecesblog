02 How to play

The rules

  • Fill every empty box with a number from 1 to 9.
  • Each row has every number from 1 to 9 exactly once.
  • So does each column…
  • …and each of the nine 3 × 3 squares (the ones with the thick borders).

Playing here

  • Click or tap a box, then type a number.
  • The arrow keys move around the grid; Backspace or Delete empties a box.
  • “Check my answers” marks any number that doesn't fit the answer.
  • Your game is saved in this browser, so you can come back to it later.
