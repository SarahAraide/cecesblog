Mind Matters

It's okay to talk about it

Cece's campaign: projects she has worked on to raise awareness about mental health.

by Cece

During my national service (שירות לאומי), I volunteered at the Maghar Youth Center, which is itself a community project. There I completed the Local Leadership Program (תוכנית מנהיגות מקומית) and created projects I believe my town needs. Mental health is the cause closest to my heart.

The projects

Instagram video · 2021

Seeing the signs

A video we made at the Maghar Youth Center to raise awareness about suicide: how to notice someone who may be struggling, how to approach them with care, and how to help them reach professional support. It also explains more about the topic, so people feel less afraid to talk about it.

Facebook video · 2021

Building Maghar's future, starting with a student house

Published by the Ministry for the Development of the Periphery, the Negev and the Galilee (המשרד לפיתוח הפריפריה, הנגב והגליל)

A video published by the Ministry for the Development of the Periphery, the Negev and the Galilee about the projects we led at the Maghar Youth Center as part of the Local Leadership Program. The one I felt most deeply is a student house for Maghar. Before I became a student, I needed a place to study, somewhere that valued my thoughts, ideas and projects. Homes in Maghar are full of life, and full of distractions, so a student house would give young people like me a space of our own.

A home full of life is also a home full of distractions.

Struggling right now?

You don't have to go through it alone.

ERAN emotional first aid (free, for any kind of emotional distress):

  • Call 1201 (24/7, anonymous, in Hebrew and other languages, including Arabic, English and Russian)
  • WhatsApp 052-8451201 (opens WhatsApp) (8:00–2:00, in Hebrew; WhatsApp isn't fully anonymous)
  • eran.org.il (opens in a new tab)

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 101 (Magen David Adom).
